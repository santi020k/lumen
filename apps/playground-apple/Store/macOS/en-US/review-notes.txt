Lumen Playground is an interactive macOS developer reference catalog. It requires no account, network connection, or special review access, and its complete functionality is available offline.

The controls labeled as demonstrations intentionally exercise component states; they do not register for notifications, download updates, submit form content, or persist personal information. Documentation, Support, and Privacy open public HTTPS pages in the system browser.

The application uses App Sandbox and requests no additional sandbox capabilities or protected-resource permissions.
