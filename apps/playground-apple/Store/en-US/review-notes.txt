Lumen Playground is a free interactive developer reference catalog. It requires no account, purchase, subscription, license key, or special review access, and its complete functionality is bundled with the app and available offline.

BUSINESS MODEL
The app does not sell, unlock, or provide access to paid digital content or services. There are no In-App Purchases, external purchases, subscriptions, paid accounts, trials, or premium features.

Answers to the Guideline 2.1(b) questions:
1. No users access paid content in the app because the app contains no paid content.
2. Users cannot purchase content for the app in the app or elsewhere.
3. The app does not recognize an account or provide access to previously purchased content.
4. No paid content, subscriptions, or features are unlocked in the app, with or without In-App Purchase.

The controls labeled as demonstrations intentionally exercise component states; they do not register for notifications, download updates, submit form content, or persist personal information. Documentation, Support, and Privacy open public HTTPS pages in the system browser.
